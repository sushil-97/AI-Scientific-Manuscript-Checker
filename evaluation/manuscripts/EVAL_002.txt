
Title
Effectiveness of Treatment B in a Randomized Clinical Trial

Abstract
A total of 150 patients were randomized.

Methods
This randomized clinical trial enrolled adult human participants.
A total of 150 patients were randomized into two treatment groups.

Results
A total of 142 patients were randomized.
Both treatment groups received the assigned intervention.

Discussion
The findings suggest that Treatment B may be effective.

Data Availability
Data are available upon reasonable request.
